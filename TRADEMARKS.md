# Name and brand

The **code** in this repository is free software under the [GNU GPL v3](LICENSE).

The **Hudhud brand** is not covered by that license. The following are © Omar Essam, all rights reserved:

- the names **Hudhud** and **هدهد** used for this software;
- the logo, the app and toolbar icons, and the hoopoe mascot in every pose
  (`extension/assets/`, `extension/icons/`, `site/assets/` and the design files they come from);
- the look of the website and the screenshots of the product.

## What you may do

- Use the name to talk about the project, link to it, review it, or say your work is "based on Hudhud".
- Ship unmodified official builds with the name and logo as they are.
- Keep the brand files in your fork of the repository while you work on contributions.

## What you may not do

- Publish a modified version, a fork, or anything built from this code **under the name Hudhud/هدهد or with its logo or mascot**. Give your version its own name and its own artwork.
- Use the name, logo or mascot in a way that suggests your product, store listing or website is the official Hudhud or is endorsed by it.

If you're unsure, open an issue and ask.
